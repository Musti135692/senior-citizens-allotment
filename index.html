About the Allotment

The Senior Citizens Allotment provides a friendly environment for members to enjoy the garden and outdoor activities with other members of the community. Whether you are an experienced gardener or just starting out in the gardening activities, the allotment is for all members of the community.

The allotment encourages healthy living by giving members the opportunity to grow fresh fruit, vegetables and flowers while sharing gardening knowledge and practical skills with others.

Gardening Tips

Water Regularly

Water plants early in the morning or during the evening to reduce evaporation and help plants stay healthy.

Grow Seasonal Vegetables

Choosing vegetables that suit the current season helps produce healthier crops and improves harvests throughout the year.

Use the Right Tools

Keeping gardening tools clean and in good condition makes gardening safer, easier and more enjoyable.

Seasonal Planting Guide

🌸 Spring

Spring is an ideal time to plant carrots, lettuce, spinach and peas. Warmer temperatures help many vegetables establish healthy growth.

☀️ Summer

During summer, tomatoes, beans, courgettes and peppers grow well with regular watering and plenty of sunlight.

🍂 Autumn

Autumn is suitable for planting onions, garlic and broad beans while preparing the allotment for the colder months.

❄️ Winter

Winter is a good time to grow hardy vegetables such as kale, winter cabbage and Brussels sprouts.
